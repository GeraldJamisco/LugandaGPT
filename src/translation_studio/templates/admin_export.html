Exports and dataset preparation

Exports are for the project owner. A reviewed translation is not training authorization. The existing training validator and provenance checks remain the final gate.

Studio review export

Download a CSV of source records, translations, review history, and dataset status. This is useful for inspection and handoff. It is not a training file.

Evaluation rows are labelled and must not be added to training data.

Training-preparation export

Download JSONL for pilot items that are approved for dataset preparation. The export checks every record against the existing training-record schema before writing.

{{ approved_count }} pilot items are approved for dataset preparation. {% if not approved_count %}Approve at least one reviewed pilot item before exporting.{% endif %}

Evaluation items are excluded. The export is a preparation artifact. It does not launch training or replace the training-data authorization gate.

What the export does not do

  • It does not change the approved benchmark specification.
  • It does not create benchmark scores or claim model quality.
  • It does not include evaluation items in the training file.
  • It does not authorize training, inference, spending, or release.
  • It does not replace provenance, consent, licence, or retention records.
