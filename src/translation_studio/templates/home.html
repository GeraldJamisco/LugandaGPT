LugandaGPT Translation Studio

Welcome to LugandaGPT Translation Studio.

We are building an AI assistant designed to understand and communicate naturally in Luganda. Your work helps us prepare and check examples that teach the system how to use the language correctly.

You will read an example in English, write its Luganda version, and save your work. Another speaker may review the translation to help us improve its accuracy and naturalness.
