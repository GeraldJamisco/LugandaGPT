Write Luganda messages

60 more everyday situations (set 2). You write the Luganda, the way people really say it. Nothing you type leaves this computer.

How to use
  1. Each page is one situation in English. Write the Luganda message the way a native speaker would really say it. Do not translate word for word.
  2. The blue box is a guide. For times and days it shows the confirmed rules, so use them as they are. For other situations it says what to watch for, for example: do not make up a date, write one sentence only, or use Luganda words where they exist.
  3. Do not use an AI to write or fix your Luganda. Your own wording is what we need.
  4. If a situation does not make sense or feels unnatural, tick "Skip" and say why in the note.
  5. At the end click Export CSV and Backup JSON, and save both in data\synthetic\ (the file names start with situations2).
