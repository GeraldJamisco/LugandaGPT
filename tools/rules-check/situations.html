Write Luganda messages

40 everyday situations. You write the Luganda, the way people really say it. Nothing you type leaves this computer.

How to use
  1. Each page is one situation in English. Write the Luganda message the way a native speaker would really say it. Do not translate word for word.
  2. The blue box shows the times and days from the rules the group already confirmed, so you do not have to work them out. Use them as they are.
  3. Do not use an AI to write or fix your Luganda. Your own wording is what we need.
  4. If a situation does not make sense or feels unnatural, tick "Skip" and say why in the note.
  5. At the end click Export CSV and Backup JSON, and save both in data\synthetic\.
